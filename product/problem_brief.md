# Product Problem Brief - Upcoming Commitments

## Problem
Current-account customers can see individual transactions, but transaction history does not automatically answer a forward-looking question: **how much of my current balance is already committed to recurring bills and subscriptions before my next payday?**

## Target user
Digitally active current-account customers who have several recurring debits and want a clearer view of near-term cash commitments without maintaining a separate spreadsheet or budgeting app.

## Jobs to be done
1. Show me which regular payments are likely to occur soon.
2. Help me understand how much money is effectively already committed.
3. Warn me when expected commitments may leave my available balance below a threshold I choose.
4. Let me correct a recurring-payment classification when the system is wrong.

## Product hypothesis
If customers receive a simple, explainable view of upcoming recurring commitments and projected available balance, they can make better short-term spending decisions with less manual tracking.

## Completed scope
- Reproducible synthetic transaction dataset.
- Explainable recurring-payment detection rules.
- Upcoming-payment forecast and projected-balance calculation.
- Interactive Streamlit application.
- PRD, customer journey, user stories/acceptance criteria, prioritised backlog, product metrics and launch-readiness checklist.
- Automated technical tests.

## Explicitly not completed
No real customer interviews, usability study, bank integration, Open Banking connection, production security review, regulatory approval or live launch has been conducted. Those are documented as next-stage validation work rather than claimed outcomes.
